  • The weekly insight is an automated rule that compares your logged calories to your goal and reports the difference. It is not a clinician, dietitian, or coach, and its suggestions carry no professional authority.
  • The water figure shown in the app is measured against a general reference of 64 fl oz per day. That reference is a rule of thumb, not a medical recommendation, and appropriate fluid intake varies with your body, activity, climate, medication, and health.
  • Talk to a doctor or a registered dietitian before you change your diet, calorie intake, or fluid intake, and especially if you are under 18, pregnant or nursing, over 65, an athlete in training, or living with diabetes, kidney disease, heart disease, a metabolic or thyroid condition, or any illness affected by diet.
